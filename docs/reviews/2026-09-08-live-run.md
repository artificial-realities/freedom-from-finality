# Live LTX-2.5 run, Director engine (2026-09-08)

Setup: a rented A100 SXM4 80 GB on Vast.ai (`server/vast_run.sh`), the base LTX-2.5 model at 15 steps with CPU offload, 768×512 at 24 fps, native audio on. Client: `configs/director.live.yaml`, scenario `data/live_scenario.json` (8 turns, one viewer-requested cut). Transcripts and clips under `logs/live*/`.

## Numbers

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Clips rendered | 8 of 8, no errors, 37 min wall time |
| Render time | 190–220 s per clip after warm-up (about 30 s per second of film); the first clip 400 s including warm-up |
| Clip length | 6–8 s, chosen by the Director |
| Voice-over | folded into the prompt as native audio on the gated segments; every clip carries an audio track |

## What worked

- Steering lands on screen every time: the soccer ball at turn 3, the close-up at turn 4, the red cap at turn 6, the bow at turn 8.
- A `cut` is a real reset. The picture restarts from the reference, the composition follows the shot spec, and the white background comes back after it had drifted.
- The figure stays a red-torso, blue-legs, yellow-head minifigure through all eight clips.

## What was wrong, and what changed

**The reference was being cropped.** `data/legoman.png` is 380×381; the server renders 768×512. The first-frame condition was scaled to fill the wider frame, which cut the head off, and the model invented a new head with hair and a printed expression the moment it pulled wide. This is visible as the first frame of every cut in the contact sheet below, and it matches the "framing drift and invented facial details" seen in the earlier spike. The client now letterboxes the seed image at the server's aspect ratio before sending it (`i2v_reference_fit: pad`, `src/i2v/ltxv.py`), probing the render size from `/health`.

**Padding colour matters.** A first attempt padded with pure white; the source's background is a faint grey gradient, so the pad left a visible rectangle and the model rendered it as a framed inset on a grey surface. The pad now continues the image's own border colour (`i2v_reference_fill: auto`), which is seamless. With that, the classic smooth head and its stud survive the whole first clip and no hair is invented.

**Cuts inside a multi-shot segment are prompt-level only.** The server reset applies to the first shot's transition; a `cut` on a second or third shot re-establishes the picture in the prompt but does not re-seed. Documented, not changed.

**A stale tunnel refuses connections silently.** One test run failed with "connection refused" because the SSH tunnel had died while the server was healthy. `vast_run.sh` now keeps the tunnel alive with a higher `ServerAliveCountMax`; check `server/vast_run.sh status` before a run.

## What remains (the identity problem, precisely)

Generation-level drift that appears in every run regardless of the reference: the head flips green with a glow around two to four seconds into a clip and sometimes back, the printed expression changes, and the white void becomes grey speckle or a spotlight. Continuous clips carry that forward; cuts bound it. This is the substrate problem the review deferred to the next phase: observation of rendered frames feeding an entity visual memory, per-character reference re-injection at cuts, and prompt tuning of the compiler's style text against LTX-2.5's own biases.
