# X-07 and X-08 — Viewer momentum, and the Showrunner's planning half while the viewer is active (gate ARCH-08, ARCH-09)

**Result:** RESULT_READY, owner review required · **Run:** 2026-09-23 · **Code:** the BATCH_017 tree · **Cost:** OpenAI only (14 runs of 40 segments with gpt-4.1 on the priority tier, plus about 330 judge calls); no GPU

## Questions and criteria (decision record §4.1)

- **X-07** — Does momentum earn its place, and what should reset it? Momentum on versus off, the same scripted lines, 40 segments, the real Director, headless. *Metric:* beats until the Director returns to a card candidate after a line; lines per scene needed to hold a direction.
- **X-08** — Is the planning half needed while the viewer is active? Showrunner memory-only versus full; candidates hidden while momentum is active versus shown; scripted lines, headless. *Criterion:* the film keeps moving when the viewer is silent; snap-back rate against stalls.

## Method

- `scripts/experiments/story_authority.py` runs the real live loop headless in real time (6.04 s clips, text only) on the Lego story with `configs/director.yaml` (gpt-4.1, the live config's priority tier), a scripted viewer that types each line when its segment starts playing (as `scripts/steer.py` does), and one of four evaluation-only arms (production code untouched): `base` (production: X-07 "on", X-08 "full"); `momentum_off` (the momentum block never reaches the Director); `memory_only` (the Director sees no arc and no scene card; the Showrunner still compacts, notes and prunes); `hide_candidates` (the card's candidate beats are hidden while a viewer direction is active). Every Director call is recorded (`plans.jsonl`: the card in force, the momentum, the line, the beat, the call's time).
- Two line schedules. *directions* (X-07): three directions at segments 5, 15 and 27 ("a small paper boat drifts in; he decides to become its captain", "everything he touches starts to bounce like rubber", "he gets sleepy and tries to build himself a bed"). *silence* (X-08): the first two, then 25 segments (about 150 s) of silence.
- Runs: base and momentum_off × 3 on *directions*; base, memory_only and hide_candidates × 2 on *silence*; memory_only and hide_candidates × 1 on *directions*. 14 runs, 336 beats.
- `scripts/experiments/judge_authority.py`: gpt-4.1 at temperature 0 (seed 7) labels each beat, given the viewer's latest direction, the card in force and the beat's clips, as `direction`, `card`, `both` or `other` (every label and its reason in `logs/experiments/story-authority/judgments.json`). Measures: per line, the beats until the first `card` beat before the next line, and the story seconds the direction holds (until the first beat that is neither `direction` nor `both`); after the last line, structural world changes per minute and the longest stretch without one; REQUIRED pacing notes; Showrunner stagnation replans.

**X-07 — momentum.** With momentum the direction held 10 s longer on average and one fewer line of nine was pulled back to the card. The runs vary a lot: the first pair differed strongly (24 s against 56 s, three returns against one), the two repetitions did not. *What resets it:* in the arms with momentum it was cleared six times by a Showrunner card landing, five of them 18 to 48 story seconds after the line (18, 24, 24, 36, 48 s; the sixth at 121 s), and the returns to the card with momentum on follow those clearings (base run 1, line at segment 28: a new card landed at segment 34, 36 s after the line, and the next five beats followed its first candidate). This is the reset the record describes: the replan that the line itself provokes erases the line's momentum. *Lines per scene:* the scripted viewer never repeats a line, so this design does not measure how many lines a direction needs; the hold time is the proxy.

**X-08 — the planning half.** Without a card (memory-only) the film moves least once the viewer is silent (0.38 structural changes per minute against 1.15 with the full Showrunner) and has the longest static stretch (151 s, past the 135 s at which the Director is told a change is required), while snap-backs cannot happen (0 of 4). Hiding candidates while a direction is active holds directions longest (104 s against 82 s on the silence schedule) with the same snap-back count (1 of 4), but the film moves less after the last line (0.57 per minute), because momentum never expires today, so the candidates stay hidden through the silence. No arm stalled into a Showrunner stagnation replan within 40 segments.

**Readings for other rows, from the production (`base`) runs.** Every beat that answered a line had at most two clips (ARCH-11: 13 of 13 answer beats in the five base runs, 4 of them one clip). Answer calls took 4.22 s on average across those runs (per-run means 2.99-5.21 s, max 6.05 s), other calls 3.51 s (session A: 2.98 s overall on the same tier, answer calls up to 4.37 s); the Director's output is the same size as in session A (1,699 against 1,678 JSON characters per beat), so the difference is not output length, and the cap's expected 0.3-0.5 s saving is not visible against the day's variation. Across all 14 runs beats had one clip 91 times, two 224, three 15 and four once (mean 10.3 s of film), inside the 6-24 s the prompt now states (ARCH-10). The Showrunner named a decision for each open thread in its rationale ("Thread t2 (paper boat captain gag) is still active and escalating, so it is continued") (ARCH-16). **ARCH-01's transcript check does not pass on the written rule alone:** in the five base runs, 7 beats followed the card while a viewer direction was still active (momentum set, 12-40 s after its line: one run's rubber ball pulled the film off the viewer's bed for six beats), and 8 more after a card landing had cleared the momentum. With the candidates hidden while a direction is active (E5), 1 beat in three runs followed the card while a direction was active (the card's goal is still shown) and 1 after a clearing.

## Limits

One story, 40-segment films (4 minutes), an LLM judge, three to nine lines per arm. The effects are of the size of the run-to-run variation except memory-only's loss of movement and the timing of the momentum clearings, which recur.

## Recommended disposition (for the owner)

- **ARCH-08 (X-07): keep momentum and fix its reset scope** as the record's "if momentum stays" branch: a card landing clears momentum only when its Showrunner run was spawned after `viewer_momentum_segment`, and momentum ages out after a configurable number of story seconds (about 90 s, the owner's Q2 answer). Evidence: the snap-backs with momentum on follow clearings 18-48 s after the line.
- **ARCH-09 (X-08): keep the planning half (reject memory-only).** Adopt E5 (hide the candidate beats while a direction is active) **together with** ARCH-08's 90-s expiry, so the candidates come back when the direction expires: E5 is what makes ARCH-01's rule hold (7 card beats against an active direction in five production runs, 1 in three E5 runs), and without the expiry it leaves the silent film with less to do.
- **ARCH-01** is implemented (both system prompts state the rule) but its validation fails on the written rule alone; it passes only with E5 and the momentum fix, i.e. after the owner's decision on ARCH-08/ARCH-09.
