# X-19 — First-clip streaming (gates ARCH-12)

**Result:** RESULT_READY · **Run:** 2026-09-24 · **Code:** `ee2cf19` + `scripts/experiments/x19_streaming.py` · **Director:** the live config's (gpt-5.4-mini, no reasoning, priority tier) · **Cost:** OpenAI calls only

The record (§4.3; review 03 R12): parse the Director's streamed `BeatPlan` and emit clip 1 as soon as its object closes, with the fields the first clip needs ordered before `clips`. Criterion: time to clip 1 and schema validity over 50 answer calls. Review 03 expected the answer path to fall from ~3.5-4 s (gpt-4.1 then) to ~1.8 s.

**Method.** One world from the live Director (the bootstrap and five quiet segments of the Lego story). Then 50 answer calls, each from a copy of it with one viewer line (ten lines in rotation). Each call ran two arms back to back:

- **Whole:** the production call, which waits for the whole beat.
- **Streamed:** the same messages, streamed, against an evaluation-only copy of `BeatPlan` with `clips` after `voiceover`, `thread_ops`, the disposition, `delivered_request` and `perspective`.

A scanner found the first clip's object in the stream (`tests/test_experiment_arms.py` checks it under arbitrary chunking). Data: `logs/experiments/x19/`.

| | Mean | p50 | p95 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whole: the answer call today | 2.69 s | 2.66 s | 3.62 s | 3.88 s |
| Streamed: first token | 0.74 s | — | 1.02 s | — |
| **Streamed: clip 1 closed** | **2.11 s** | **1.99 s** | 3.39 s | 4.67 s |
| Streamed: the whole reply | 3.02 s | 2.81 s | 4.14 s | 7.07 s |

- **Validity:**
  - 50 of 50 streamed replies parse as a `BeatPlan` with no validator violations.
  - 50 of 50 first clips parse alone as a `ClipSpec` when they close.
  - Every line was absorbed in both arms.
  - Answer beats had 1.9-2.0 clips.
- **Time to clip 1:** 1.99 s median, near review 03's 1.8 s.
- **The saving:**
  - Within a streamed reply, clip 1 is ready 0.86 s (median) before the reply ends, at 69 % of it.
  - Against the paired whole call it is ready 0.76 s sooner at the median (0.58 s mean), and sooner in 42 of 50 calls.
  - The streamed replies ran 50 tokens longer on average (585 against 535).
- **Why the saving is modest:** review 03's estimate was made on gpt-4.1's 3.5-4 s answers. Today's Director answers in 2.7 s, and answer beats are capped at two clips (ARCH-11), so clip 1 is about two thirds of the reply.

## Recommended disposition (for the owner)

**Defer ARCH-12 ("not now", the record's alternative).** Streaming the first clip saves about 0.6-0.9 s per answer call. It needs:
- `BeatPlan`'s field order changed;
- a streaming path in the OpenAI backend;
- the engine applying and emitting clip 1 before the beat is whole. The validator's re-ask cannot take back a clip already emitted, so clip 1 would need its own check.

With gpt-5.4-mini, film B of GPU session 4 already passes every gate with a 2.31-s lowest margin and no stall. The saving would matter if the buffer were lowered to land lines sooner, which is RUN-06's question (rejected as specified, D-01..D-04).
