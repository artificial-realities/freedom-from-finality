# X-26 — ARCH-19's cast and perspective policy in a run (post-implementation validation)

**State:** LLM half run 2026-09-23; the identity half (GPU) pending · **Code:** the BATCH_020 tree

The record's method: a story with one fixed protagonist, one predefined supporting character and one dynamically introduced character, with a permitted and a forbidden perspective switch; count validator retries and inspect identity, voice and state persistence over 20+ clips. Criterion: protagonist invariants never drift; permitted characters can enter and remain stable; forbidden cast/perspective changes are rejected; retry rate operationally acceptable.

## Run

`scripts/experiments/story_authority.py --arm base --schedule cast --story tests/fixtures/lighthouse --segments 32`: the real Director (gpt-4.1, priority tier), headless, real time, on the synthetic lighthouse story (protagonist Mara; Tomas, who *must* appear when the lamp fails for the first time; new characters allowed as adults from the sea or the village; first-person allowed only through the telescope). Lines at segments 4 ("a fisherman staggers in"), 9 ("the lamp sputters and goes dark"), 15 ("Mara looks through the telescope"), 21 ("show the tower from high above, a bird's-eye view"), 26 ("the fisherman asks Mara for a blanket"). 30 clips played. `logs/experiments/x26/base-cast-0/`.

## Findings (LLM half)

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| A new character is registered once with what keeps it the same | **Yes**: `fisherman` (dynamic) at segment 6 with a role ("fisherman caught in the storm"), `speaks`, a voice ("rough, weary, local accent"), a look and a short look carried on later clips, and its state (standing, asking Mara for a blanket, right of frame) |
| The protagonist's state is tracked | Yes: Mara's posture, activity and position follow the clips |
| A permitted perspective switch | **Used**: `perspective: first-person` on the telescope beat (segment 17) |
| A forbidden perspective change is refused | **Not exercised**: the Director read "a bird's-eye view" as a high camera within third-person (a framing, which the story allows), so the validator had nothing to refuse; the policy's terms are perspectives, not camera heights |
| A supporting character that must appear enters when its condition is met | **No**: the lamp went dark at segment 10 and Tomas had not entered by segment 30 (two minutes later); nothing makes the Showrunner act on the condition beyond its instructions |
| Retry rate | 2 validator re-asks in 24 Director calls (8 %), neither about the cast or perspective policy (an unknown `lamp`, a towel as a holder); both corrected |
| Identity persistence on screen | GPU half, pending |

A cosmetic fix came out of it: a generated short look no longer ends in a comma.

## Note for the owner

The "must appear" condition is advisory in ARCH-19 as approved (the Showrunner decides when an entrance serves the story). If the owner wants such conditions honoured reliably, a deterministic signal (the Showrunner told how long a must-appear condition has been met) would be a new row; it is not implemented.
