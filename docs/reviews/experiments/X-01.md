**State:** BLOCKED on listeners (the material is ready) · **Prepared:** 2026-09-23

The record's method: 20 cross-clip pairs from session A plus 5 same-clip controls, blind, at least three listeners. Success: ≥ 90 % of cross-clip pairs judged "same"; controls ≥ 95 %.

`scripts/experiments/x01_pairs.py` has built the material from session A's 15 narrated clips, using review 04's ASR speech spans: `logs/experiments/x01/listen/` holds `pair_01.wav` … `pair_25.wav` (20 cross-clip pairs and 5 controls, the two halves of one clip's line, in a seeded random order), a `README.txt` for the listener and a `responses.csv` to fill in; `logs/experiments/x01/answer_key.json` maps each file to its clips and must stay closed until everyone has answered.

**What the owner needs to do:** give the `listen` folder to three or more people, collect their `responses.csv`, and score them against the key (the share of cross-clip pairs answered "yes", and of controls).
