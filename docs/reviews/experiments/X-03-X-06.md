## X-03 — the 153-frame shape (gate: LTX-12's shape; RUN-04)

*Criterion:* render p95 ≤ 0.85, max ≤ 0.92; no frame-difference spike at the seam above intra-clip motion; A/V aligned. 30 clips each (segments 1-30), one key, warm = clips 4-30.

| Arm | Air time used for the ratio | Wall ratio p50 / p95 / max | Server-side (generate + encode) p50 / p95 / max | Generate median | Encode median |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 145 frames (today's shape; today's player airs all 145) | 6.042 s | 0.811 / **0.880** / 0.904 | 0.760 / 0.819 / 0.846 | 3.57 s | 1.07 s |
| 153 frames (144 new + the 9-frame overlap, aired as 144) | 6.000 s | 0.859 / **0.912** / 0.913 | 0.807 / 0.847 / 0.847 | 3.66 s | 1.13 s |

- **Cost.** 153 frames cost 0.23 s more per clip on the wall (median 5.16 s against 4.93 s): +0.09 s generate, +0.07 s encode. Per second of *new* picture under the overlap player (145 frames give 136 new, 5.67 s), 153 is slightly cheaper: 0.86 against 0.87.
- **Gates.** On this box **both shapes fail the p95 gate on the wall clock** (0.880 and 0.912 against ≤ 0.85); both pass max ≤ 0.92. Server-side both pass (0.819, 0.847). The wall adds about 0.31 s per clip of client overhead even on localhost (median; from 0.29 to 0.40 s), and the CPU encode is 1.0-1.3 s, about 1.6 times the 2026-09-16 NVL probe's per-frame encode (0.57 s for 121 frames). Session A's stage timings were not recorded, so this box cannot be compared with session A's stage by stage. See also the warm probe below.
- **Seam.** Measured where the overlap player cuts (N's frame n − w − 1 against N+1's frame 0, which repeats N's frame n − w), in absolute terms (0-255): the join is 0.3-0.9, the codec's floor, at 21 of 29 seams (145) and 24 of 29 (153); the others follow fast motion inside N. A spike here is a join above 1.5 and above twice N's own frame step just before the cut: two small ones in each arm (145: seams 1 and 14, 3.4 and 2.8; 153: seams 18 and 28, 2.2 and 2.3, against local motion of 0.1-0.6), and one large one in the 153 arm, seam 20 (19.3): during session A's breakdance the 153-frame chain dissolved the figure into a blurred headspin (`figures/x03-seam20.png`) and the next clip did not reproduce the blur (overlap error 13.9 against 0.3-1.8 elsewhere); the 145-frame chain on the same prompts spun on one foot and stayed recognisable. Chaotic motion, not the shape, is the likely cause (review 02 §2.3; LTX-04's plausibility rule came after session A), but the case is one seam.
- **A/V.** Without padding the audio track is shorter than the video by a constant 31.7 ms at 145 frames and 3.3 ms at 153 (under a frame either way). With AUD-03's candidate (`LTX2_AUDIO_CEILING_LUFS=-18`, six clips of each shape after a restart) audio and video are the same length (0.0 ms) and the ceiling acts as designed: loud narrated clips from −9.8/−9.4 LUFS to −18.0 with true peak from 0.0 to −8.2 dBTP; quiet clips untouched.
