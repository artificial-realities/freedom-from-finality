## Adversarial phase (turns 91–115)

Declined with an in-world alternative: dialogue from the mute figure (waved instead), a second minifigure, on-screen text, the void turning blue. Reinterpreted within the rules: the kitchen (a box and a banana), bending knees (rigid descent), tears (a blue "tear" brick), eating the pie (smeared). Growing ten feet tall was honoured as a stacking gag, which stretches "LEGO anatomy is absolute"; acceptable as comedy, worth a rule if it matters.

## Changes made after the run

- The opening scene summary is now pinned in every Director prompt; the start of a film is what viewers refer back to.
- The world section is bounded: the character plus the 12 most recently seen props in full, the rest by id, status and look clipped to 120 characters, the gone list capped.
- The compiled video prompt re-identifies at most 6 on-screen entities with their look; the rest by name. It had grown from ~290 to ~440 words as props accumulated.
- The Director is told that something the viewer calls pre-existing but absent from World now does not exist: let it appear as new, or search and find nothing. It is also told to declare `scene_done` as soon as the exit condition is met; the cap is a backstop.

## Verdict

The properties the redesign was for hold at 180 segments: a correct, complete prop ledger, bounded context, a plan that advances (20 scenes, 42 paid-off threads, three-act arc), rule traps caught structurally, and viewer input recorded with a disposition every time. The two misses were retrieval of the very beginning (fixed by pinning) and a phantom prop asserted by the viewer (prompt guidance added). The legacy four-role engine, its prompts and the Mem0 path can be removed.
